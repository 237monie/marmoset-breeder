How It Started

Where Our Love for Marmosets Began

Our love for marmosets began with a simple fascination that quickly grew into something much more meaningful.

Their playful personalities, curious nature, and gentle little faces captured our hearts. Over time, caring for them became more than a passion, it became a part of our family.

Today, we are dedicated to raising happy, healthy, and well-socialized marmosets and helping loving families welcome one into their lives.
